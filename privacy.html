2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Feedr Forums may collect or process the following information:

  • Email address: Used when creating and managing an account.
  • Username: Used to identify your account and allow you to log in.
  • Password and authentication information: Used to authenticate your account. Authentication is handled through Supabase Auth.
  • Profile information: This can include your display name, pronouns and bio if you choose to provide them.
  • Account information: Information such as your account ID and account creation details may be processed to operate your account.
  • Technical information: Our service providers may process technical information needed to operate, maintain and secure the service.

We do not ask users to provide unnecessary personal information in their profiles.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Allowing users to log in securely.
  • Displaying usernames and profile information.
  • Saving profile changes.
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of Feedr Forums.
  • Responding to support and privacy requests.
  • Meeting legal obligations where applicable.

We do not intend to use account information for personalised advertising or sell personal information. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the new use begins.

4. Why We Are Allowed to Process Information

Under UK data protection law, we need a lawful reason for processing personal information.

Depending on the activity, this may include:

  • Providing the service: Processing information needed to create and operate your account.
  • Legitimate interests: Processing information where necessary to maintain security, prevent misuse and keep Feedr Forums working, while considering users' rights and interests.
  • Legal obligations: Processing or retaining information where the law requires us to do so.
  • Consent: Where consent is the appropriate legal basis, we will ask for it and explain how it can be withdrawn.

5. Third-Party Services and Supabase

Feedr Forums uses Supabase to provide backend services, including account authentication and database storage.

This means information such as your email address, username, profile information and account data is processed using Supabase's services.

Supabase may also process technical information necessary to provide and secure its services.

Supabase has its own privacy policy explaining how it handles information: Supabase Privacy Policy .

We do not control every aspect of Supabase's infrastructure or its processing activities. Its role and responsibilities are governed by the relevant agreements and applicable data protection law.

6. Images and User Content

Feedr Forums is intended to allow users to create and share content as its features develop.

If image uploads or other content-sharing features become available, the information contained in uploaded content may be stored and processed to provide those features.

We will update this Privacy Policy before introducing new features that change what personal information is collected or how it is used.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information, private contact details or information about other people without their permission.

7. Browser Storage and Preferences

Feedr Forums currently uses browser storage to remember certain preferences, such as your selected theme and text size.

Supabase authentication may also use browser storage to maintain your signed-in session.

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings, although doing so may reset preferences or sign you out.

8. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Supabase where necessary to operate Feedr Forums.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of users or the service.

9. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes explained in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Account information may be retained while an account remains active. Information may also remain in backups or records for a limited period after deletion where necessary for security, legal or technical reasons.

We will review our retention practices as Feedr Forums develops and introduce clearer retention periods for different types of information.

10. Keeping Information Safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no website or online service can guarantee complete security. Users should keep their passwords private and avoid sharing account credentials.
